I built a tool that reads a CV the way a hiring manager does. It's called Seera (سيرة).

You drop in a CV (PDF, Word, text, or even a photo) and within about a minute you get:

📊 An overall strength score out of 100, with a plain verdict like "Strong base, undersold"
👀 A "6-second recruiter scan": what someone notices first when they skim the page
🎯 A breakdown across 5 areas: impact, clarity, skills relevance, experience depth, and how well hiring systems (ATS) can read it
✅ Strengths and weaknesses, each quoted from the CV itself, with a concrete fix for every weakness
💬 10 interview questions this specific CV is likely to trigger, including the uncomfortable ones about gaps and vague claims

The part I'm proudest of is the questions. Each one shows why an interviewer would ask it, what a strong answer covers, how the answer should sound, a sample answer, and what a weak answer sounds like.

A few design decisions:
• Only a drop box at the start. You don't have to fill in a form first.
• Optional "Hiring for a specific role?" field. Paste a job ad and the score and questions adapt to it. You can also re-run the analysis against a new role without uploading again.
• Works with Arabic and English CVs.
• You can view the original CV inside the tool, next to its report.
• The file is read in the browser and never stored.

Who it's for:
→ Job seekers who want to know how their CV lands before they hit "Apply", and to prepare for the interview it will lead to
→ Recruiters and hiring managers who want a structured first read and a ready-made question list
